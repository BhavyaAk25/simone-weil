# Guitar auditions

Original audition arrangements for this project, using FreePats Spanish classical guitar samples by Roberto (roberto@zenvoid.org), version 2019-06-18, recorded in 2008. Samples are published under CC0 1.0.

Source: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/acoustic-guitar.html
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Modifications: softened attacks, high-frequency reduction, low-frequency cleanup, quiet room reflections, sparse original note arrangements, level matching and fades. No documented personal music preference is attributed to Simone Weil.
